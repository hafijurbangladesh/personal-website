## Context

Government decision-making runs on files ("nothi"): records that move between desks and offices for notes, review and approval. Paper files are slow to move, hard to track and difficult to search. A national e-filing system had to digitise this core administrative workflow for offices across government, in a way that could scale to a very large number of users and offices.

## My Role

As Senior Software Engineer at a2i, I led the **architecture, database design and core framework** of E-Nothi, the system later known as D-Nothi.

## What was built

E-Nothi provided the foundation for electronic file management in government offices. My responsibility was the technical core that everything else depended on:

- **Architecture**: the overall structure of the system, designed for government-wide use
- **Database design**: the data model behind files, notes and their movement between offices and officials
- **Core framework**: the shared foundation on which the application's features were built

Getting these foundations right mattered because an e-filing system becomes part of an organisation's daily operations, and its data model and framework must support growth over many years.

## Outcome

D-Nothi has grown into a national platform with **150,000+ users across 19,000+ offices**, making it one of the most widely used digital government systems in Bangladesh.
