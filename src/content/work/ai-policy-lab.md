## Context

Governments increasingly want to use AI to strengthen policy and public administration, but they need safe ways to experiment first. A policy lab with a proper sandbox lets institutions test AI applications on real problems, build internal capability and apply responsible-AI safeguards before anything goes into production.

## My Role

As Technology Specialist with the Digital and AI Hub at UNDP Bangladesh, I **designed the technical setup, sandbox and prototype architecture** for the AI Policy Lab in the **Cabinet Division**. I also provided **architecture review, responsible-AI advisory and quality assurance**.

## What was built

- **Technical setup** for the lab environment
- **A sandbox** in which AI prototypes can be built and tested safely
- **Prototype architecture** for the lab's AI applications, including:
  - the **AI Project Analyser**
  - the **AI-based Policy Performance Analyzer**

During the same role I also contributed to the **Digital Service Delivery Standards**.

## Outcome

The AI Policy Lab gives the Cabinet Division a technical foundation for exploring AI in policy work, with prototypes that make the possibilities concrete and responsible-AI advisory built in from the start. It connects to my broader focus on institutional AI capability and AI governance in government.
